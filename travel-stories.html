Field note 01

Paris, France

Eiffel Tower

The iron lattice tower was built for the 1889 Paris World’s Fair. From the Trocadéro, its four legs and the great arches between them are easy to pick out against the skyline.

Look closer: Notice how the legs curve inward on the way up.

Field note 02

Rome, Italy

Colosseum

The first-century Flavian Amphitheatre hosted public spectacles, including gladiatorial games. Its rows of stone arches are one of the easiest ways to read the building from the street.

Look closer: Watch how the pattern of the arches changes from one level to the next.

Field note 03

New York, USA

Statue of Liberty

Dedicated in 1886, the copper statue stands on Liberty Island in New York Harbor. The National Park Service lists the authorized ferry operator and explains the different ticket options.

Look closer: From the ferry, look for the torch above the island’s tree line.
